# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Communicating respectfully and considering different perspectives.
- Giving and receiving feedback constructively.
- Sharing only information that is appropriate for a public project.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of credentials, customer data, or private operational details.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository host's contact options.
